A few useful
things to know.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Your dates, number of travellers, any children’s ages, where you’ll be starting from and the experiences that interest you. Let us know about access or other practical requirements so we can check suitable options.

Are transport, meals and entry fees included?

Inclusions vary by itinerary. Ask for a written quotation showing transport, any meals, equipment, guide arrangements and entry fees, along with any additional charges.

Are wildlife sightings or marine activities guaranteed?

Wildlife sightings vary, and marine activities depend on conditions and provider arrangements. We’ll help clarify the proposed itinerary and what happens if an activity needs to change.

Can I combine accommodation and experiences?

Yes, tell us which stay or area you have in mind and what you’d like to do. We’ll check how the plans fit together and confirm availability and arrangements before you book.
