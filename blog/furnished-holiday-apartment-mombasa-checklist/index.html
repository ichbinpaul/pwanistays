What to check before booking a furnished holiday apartment in Mombasa

PwaniStays · 21 November 2026

A furnished holiday apartment in Mombasa should fit how you plan to spend your days. A good shortlist starts with five things you can confirm in writing: the exact location, the sleeping setup, the included facilities, the total price and the check-in arrangements.

1. Get the exact location pin

Ask for the building entrance pin and one point you will use often, such as the beach entrance, supermarket or meeting place. Compare the routes at the times you expect to travel. If you arrive late, ask where a driver should stop and whether someone will be there to receive you. If you plan to walk, ask what the last part of the route is like and whether it is suitable for your group.

2. Match the rooms and beds to your group

Ask what beds are in each room and whether the lounge doubles as sleeping space. If you need a cot, extra bedding or a room for someone who avoids stairs, confirm that before paying. Ask for current photos of the specific unit when a listing shows pictures from several apartments in one building.

3. Check the amenities you will rely on

  • Meals. If you plan to cook, ask which appliances, cookware and plates are available. A kitchenette may be enough for breakfast but not for preparing group dinners.
  • Wi-Fi and power. For remote work, ask for recent connection information, a place to work and details of backup power if uninterrupted calls matter.
  • Cooling and laundry. Ask which rooms have air conditioning or fans. If you are staying longer, check whether laundry is in the unit, shared or arranged separately.
  • Pool and parking. Confirm the pool hours, guest access rules and whether there is a reserved parking space. If you are visiting with children, ask about pool supervision and balcony security.

4. Compare the written total

Ask for the total for your dates and number of guests, with cleaning, utilities, extra guest charges and any optional services listed separately. Confirm when deposits and balances are due and how changes or cancellations are handled. The cheapest advertised nightly rate may lead to a higher total once the details are included.

5. Confirm how you will check in

Keep the arrival instructions, named contact and accepted payment method in one place. Ask what happens if your flight or train is delayed, and who to call if the unit or check-in instructions don’t match what you agreed. Save the details somewhere you can open even with limited signal.

A short message you can send

“I’m looking for a furnished apartment for [dates] for [guests]. Please confirm the exact pin, the beds in each room, the amenities included, the full price and how check-in works. We expect to arrive around [time].”
