How to choose a coastal villa in Kenya for a group trip

PwaniStays · 23 November 2026

Start with the names and needs of the people traveling before you compare villas. A villa may sleep the whole group on paper while still falling short on bathrooms, dining space or room privacy. Confirm the details that will matter on an ordinary day, especially breakfast, getting around and where everyone will sleep.

Map the rooms before dividing the bill

Ask for a list of the beds and bathrooms by room. Mark which travelers will share and who needs a ground-floor room, cot or private bathroom. Check whether extra beds are included and whether a living-room bed would reduce the group’s shared space. Before dividing the bill, agree whether you’re splitting by person or by bedroom. Check what happens to the quote if a household drops out or an extra guest joins.

Check the space you will use together

Ask whether everyone can eat at the same table, whether the kitchen has enough equipment and whether a cook or chef can be arranged if you want one. Ask who shops and pays for the groceries, which meals the quote covers, and who handles cleanup. If you plan a group dinner, confirm the dining capacity and the rules for daytime visitors before inviting anyone. If the group needs time apart, a terrace or second sitting room may matter as much as another large bedroom.

Check who can use the pool and when. Ask about depth, supervision arrangements, children’s access and whether it is shared. For a family group, ask about stairs, balcony railings and whether rooms can be locked separately. Tailor the questions to the children and adults who will actually be there.

Choose the location around your group’s plans

Shortlist a location based on what the group wants most. A group sharing meals and spending most of its time at the villa can compare a different set of options from friends who expect to go out each evening. Ask for the entrance pin, how many vehicles can park, and how a driver would find the property after dark. If some guests want beach time while others stay at the villa, check the route from the gate to the nearest public beach access and whether you’ll need a second vehicle.

Compare transfers from the arrival points your group will actually use. For a Diani villa, check flights to Ukunda. For Watamu, include Malindi Airport when comparing options. If people arrive at different times, price separate pickups alongside one shared vehicle and check that there is luggage space. Ask whether the first guests can check in for the group and arrange access for anyone arriving late. Safarilink Diani airport information · Kenya Wildlife Service guide to reaching Watamu

Request a group quote in writing

Compare rates using the same dates and guest count. Ask what is included in the total and whether cleaning, utilities, a chef, extra guests or other services cost more. Confirm the deposit, balance date, cancellation terms and what happens if part of the group no longer travels.

A good next step is to give your host one summary with the headcount, sleeping needs, dates, arrival times and two or three must-have facilities. Then ask for an itemised quote and the name of your check-in contact.
