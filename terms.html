Booking Terms & Conditions

Understand how quotations, reservations and guest responsibilities work when you book through PwaniStays.

1. Our role

PwaniStays connects guests with accommodation owners and hosts, helps manage inquiries and may coordinate reservations as a booking agent. Unless we expressly agree otherwise in writing, the property owner or host provides the accommodation and sets property-specific availability, house rules and some service conditions. We may also separately agree to manage certain properties.

2. Listings and inquiries

Photos, amenities and descriptions are provided to help you choose. They may change, and individual features must be confirmed before booking. An inquiry, displayed listing, selected date or message is not a confirmed reservation or a promise that a unit is available.

3. Quotations and booking confirmation

After receiving your dates and guest details, we confirm availability with the host and send a written quotation. It will identify the accommodation, dates, number of guests, total accommodation price, any compulsory charges, the payment recipient, the deposit and balance due, and the applicable cancellation terms. A reservation becomes confirmed only when the required deposit has been paid and PwaniStays or the host confirms the booking in writing. An inquiry or unpaid quotation does not reserve dates.

4. Prices, deposit and balance

Accommodation prices vary by season, duration and property; your written quotation states the applicable total. Our standard payment arrangement is 50% of the accommodation total as a reservation deposit, and the remaining 50% payable once the guest has checked in and accessed the property. Once your reservation is confirmed, the 50% deposit is non-refundable if you choose to cancel. If you voluntarily cancel before check-in, you do not ordinarily owe the unpaid balance under the standard arrangement. Any alternative host-specific schedule must be disclosed and agreed before payment.

We disclose known compulsory extras before payment, including cleaning fees, air-conditioning or electricity charges and any separate refundable damage deposit. Pay only via verified official payment instructions and retain your receipt. Your deposit secures the booking; it does not guarantee a refund if your plans change. See the Cancellation & Refund Policy for details and the exceptions that protect legal consumer rights.

5. Guest responsibilities

Guests must provide accurate information about dates, occupancy and relevant special requests, follow the property-specific house rules, respect neighbours and local law, and take reasonable care of the premises. Additional guests, pets, parties or events may require written approval. A guest may be responsible for proven damage caused during their stay, subject to fair assessment and applicable law.

6. Changes, cancellations and problems

Read our Cancellation & Refund Policy, including the non-refundable 50% deposit on guest cancellations. Changes to dates, group size or accommodation require host approval and reconfirmation, and may affect the price. If the booked property becomes unavailable or materially differs from what was agreed, contact us promptly. The guest-cancellation deposit rule does not remove your rights to an appropriate remedy where the service is not provided as agreed.

7. Responsibility and consumer rights

We aim to communicate accurately and facilitate a reliable booking experience. Each party remains responsible for the services and obligations it provides. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Kenyan law.

8. Contact and disputes

For booking questions or complaints, contact bookings@pwanistays.com, +254 738 544 784, or our official WhatsApp line. Please include your booking reference and a brief explanation. We will make reasonable efforts to resolve the concern before any formal dispute process.
