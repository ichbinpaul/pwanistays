How to book your Kenya stay.

Choose a coastal apartment, villa or experience. Our local team checks availability, sends a written quote and helps you plan your arrival.

See the booking steps ↓ Plan a custom trip

From inquiry to check-in

  1. Share your plans

    Browse our coastal stays or Kenya experiences. Tell us your dates, group size and what you have in mind.

  2. Review your quote

    We check availability and confirm the details, what is included and the total price in writing before you book.

  3. Reserve your dates

    For a standard accommodation booking, pay a 50% deposit to secure your reservation and the balance after check-in. Review your quote and the cancellation policy before paying.

  4. Arrive with local support

    We send arrival and check-in details. You can reach the local team if you need help during your stay.
