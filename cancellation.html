Cancellation & Refund Policy

Understand the 50% reservation deposit, balance at check-in and cancellation terms before you book.

At a glance: Our standard accommodation booking requires a 50% deposit to secure the reservation, with the remaining 50% payable once the guest has checked in and accessed the property. Once the booking is confirmed, the deposit is non-refundable if the guest cancels, regardless of how far in advance the cancellation is made. Different property-specific conditions apply only when disclosed and agreed before payment. Mandatory consumer rights remain protected.

1. Inquiries and unconfirmed reservations

Requesting accommodation rates or availability is free and does not create a booking. We first confirm the property's availability, the total quoted price, the payment recipient and the applicable terms in writing. If a deposit is received but the reservation cannot be confirmed, it will be returned in full. No guest cancellation penalty applies before a reservation is confirmed.

2. Standard payment arrangement

  • 50% deposit on reservation: Unless your written quotation states different terms, you pay 50% of the agreed accommodation total to secure your dates. Once PwaniStays or the host confirms the reservation in writing, this deposit is non-refundable on a guest-initiated cancellation because dates are reserved and the host may be paid in advance.
  • Remaining 50% after check-in: You may access the property and complete check-in before paying the remaining balance. The balance is payable once you have checked in. Any extra charges, refundable security deposits, or alternative instalments must be stated in your quotation before you pay.
  • Payment verification: Use only payment details confirmed through PwaniStays' official contact channels. Request and retain payment confirmation and your booking reference.

3. If the guest cancels before check-in

The 50% reservation deposit is not refunded for a guest-initiated cancellation of a confirmed booking, including when the guest cancels well before the arrival date. Under our standard 50/50 arrangement, the unpaid check-in balance is not ordinarily charged when you cancel before check-in. If you have already paid more than the deposit, the amount above the non-refundable deposit will normally be returned, subject to any additional, clearly disclosed and agreed non-refundable charges and applicable law.

Example: For accommodation quoted at KSh 20,000, a KSh 10,000 deposit confirms the booking. If you voluntarily cancel before check-in under the standard terms, the KSh 10,000 deposit is retained. If you had already paid the entire KSh 20,000 in advance, KSh 10,000 would ordinarily be refundable.

4. No-shows, late arrival and early departure

If you do not arrive for a confirmed booking, the deposit remains non-refundable. Under standard terms, the unpaid balance is not automatically charged for a no-show unless an additional obligation was clearly agreed before payment. If you check in and later shorten your stay or depart early for personal reasons, the agreed accommodation charges for the booked stay are generally not refundable. Notify us promptly if you anticipate a change; any voluntary date credit or adjustment requires host approval.

5. Date changes and exceptional requests

Changes to dates, property or group size depend on host approval and may affect the rate. A request to change dates does not itself cancel the original booking or guarantee a transfer of the deposit. We may ask the host to consider a date change or credit as a goodwill exception, but this is not guaranteed and must be confirmed in writing.

6. Property-specific and holiday terms

Some independent hosts, holiday dates, group reservations or special offers may require a different deposit, payment deadline or cancellation arrangement. We will identify any different terms in your written quotation before you make payment. We will not impose undisclosed stricter conditions after confirmation. The terms agreed for your reservation apply, subject always to applicable law.

7. If PwaniStays or the host cannot provide the agreed accommodation

The guest-cancellation deposit rule does not apply where the confirmed accommodation cannot be supplied by the responsible provider or there is a material failure to deliver what was agreed. We will work to arrange an acceptable alternative or an appropriate refund, including a full refund of payments for undelivered accommodation where required. If payment was made directly to a host, we will assist in pursuing the refund from the payment recipient. You are not required to accept a materially different property in place of the booking you paid for.

8. How to cancel and when refunds are processed

Send your cancellation request to bookings@pwanistays.com or WhatsApp +254 738 544 784 with your name, property, dates and booking reference. We will acknowledge the request and explain the applicable terms and any balance to be refunded in writing. Where PwaniStays is responsible for making an approved refund, we aim to process it within 14 business days, subject to any shorter legal deadline. Payment-provider processing may add time.

9. Your legal rights

Our non-refundable deposit applies to voluntary guest cancellations of confirmed accommodation reservations; it does not remove remedies for unavailable or materially misrepresented accommodation or any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. We handle disputes and requests for legally required remedies on their individual facts.
