Privacy Policy

How PwaniStays handles personal information when you enquire about accommodation or travel services.

1. Who we are

PwaniStays is a brand operated by Pwanira Ventures in Kenya. For privacy requests, contact bookings@pwanistays.com or call +254 738 544 784.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us, we may receive your name, email, telephone number, country, travel dates, group size, accommodation preferences and the message you send. If you book, we may process necessary reservation, payment and check-in details. If you choose to submit a public review, we collect the review details you enter. We do not ask for sensitive medical details in the ordinary accommodation inquiry process.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to inquiries, verify availability, prepare quotations and coordinate reservations.
  • To communicate changes, service arrangements and check-in information.
  • To keep required financial and operational records, resolve issues and prevent misuse.
  • To publish guest reviews only after moderation, where submitted for that purpose.

Depending on the activity, our lawful grounds may include taking steps at your request before a contract, performance of a booking, legal obligations, legitimate business interests or consent where required.

4. Who receives information

Information needed to fulfil a stay may be shared with the relevant property owner, host or service provider. Our website uses Supabase for database services, Web3Forms for contact form delivery and WhatsApp when you choose to contact us there. Those services may process information under their own terms, and data may be processed outside Kenya subject to applicable safeguards.

5. Storage, retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the relevant booking, customer service, recordkeeping, disputes and legal obligations. We use access controls and other practical safeguards; however, no online service is entirely risk-free. Do not submit passwords, card PINs or other unnecessary sensitive information in inquiry forms.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about the use of your personal data, access it, object to processing, and request correction or deletion where appropriate. Email bookings@pwanistays.com with a clear description of your request. You may also contact Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

7. Website tools and external links

Some website features may remember language preferences in your browser. External links, social media and messaging services are managed by their respective providers and have their own policies.

8. Changes

We may update this notice when our services or legal requirements change. The version published on this page is the current website notice.

Questions? Contact bookings@pwanistays.com or WhatsApp +254 738 544 784.
