How booking works.

Plan a coastal stay or a Kenya experience with our local team. Share your dates and preferences, then review availability, pricing and arrangements before confirming.

Plan your trip See the booking steps ↓

From enquiry to arrival

  1. 1

    Share your plans

    Choose a stay or experience and tell us your dates, group size and preferences.

  2. 2

    Review your quote

    Our team checks availability and shares a written quote with prices and what’s included.

  3. 3

    Confirm your booking

    For most stays, a 50% deposit secures the reservation. The balance is due at check-in before property access.

  4. 4

    Get ready to arrive

    We’ll share check-in or meeting instructions and stay available if you need help during your trip.
