Haller Park or Nguuni? How to choose a Mombasa nature outing

PwaniStays · 28 November 2026

If you’re staying in Nyali or Shanzu and have half a day for a nature outing, choose Haller Park for a walk with several wildlife stops or Nguuni Nature Sanctuary for a slower visit with time for a picnic. Both are in the Bamburi area, so give your driver the full name and the entrance pin when you book a ride.

Haller Park for a walk with wildlife stops

Haller Park grew out of a rehabilitated limestone quarry. Its attractions include forest paths, the reptile enclosure and a raised giraffe viewing platform. With young children, pick two things they’re most interested in and leave time for breaks. If feeding or a particular attraction is the main reason you’re going, confirm its timing and access with the park before deciding when to arrive. Haller Park’s wildlife and trail guide · How the quarry was restored

If someone uses a pushchair or mobility aid, ask the park which paths connect the stops you’ve chosen and whether the viewing platform has step-free access. Also ask about seating along that route. Use the Haller Park entrance off the Mombasa–Malindi road for pickup and drop-off. Haller Park visitor information

Nguuni for a picnic and a slower pace

Nguuni has picnic spots in the sanctuary. Ask where you can set up, how far it is from the reception point and what to bring. For a late-afternoon visit, agree on the pickup time before you go in. If you hope to see wildlife, ask staff where viewing is allowed and follow their guidance on how much distance to keep. Nguuni Nature Sanctuary

The sanctuary is off Kiembeni Road. It recommends arriving by taxi or tuk-tuk and describes the internal roads as safari roads. If you’re taking your own car, ask about the current road conditions and where to park. If you’re getting dropped off with picnic bags, confirm whether the driver can collect you from the same point. Nguuni’s directions and visitor guidance

Work out the pickup before choosing both

Start with one stop if you want to be back for lunch or a rest at your accommodation. For a longer outing, check the two entrance pins and ask the driver to quote the trip in three legs: your accommodation to the first site, between the sites, and back. Check admission and any activity charges separately with each site. Bring water, closed shoes and insect repellent.

If you’re choosing where to stay, use your accommodation’s actual pin to compare the drive to your preferred entrance. The two outings can work from either Nyali or Shanzu.
