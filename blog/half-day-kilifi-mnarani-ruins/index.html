How to spend half a day in Kilifi visiting Mnarani Ruins

PwaniStays · 25 November 2026

A half day is enough to visit Mnarani Ruins and stop for lunch around Kilifi Creek. Start with the ruins while it’s cooler, then pick one place to eat or spend time by the water. If you’re driving through from Mombasa, it also works as a single stop on the way north.

Start on the Mnarani side of Kilifi Creek

Mnarani Ruins sit on high ground on the south bank of the creek. Coming from Mombasa, aim for the monument before crossing Kilifi Bridge. Coming from Kilifi town, you’ll cross the bridge to reach it. Use the pin for Mnarani Ruins or Mnarani National Monument, and confirm it with your driver. The name Mnarani refers to the surrounding area as well as the monument. Enzi Museum’s description of Mnarani

If you’re getting dropped off, agree on a pickup point and time before the driver leaves. Share your lunch or beach pin too. The site overlooking the creek, a restaurant across the bridge and a beach entrance are three separate stops.

What to look for at the ruins

At the former Swahili settlement you can see the remains of two mosques, tombs and large baobabs. At the larger mosque, look for the mihrab, a niche indicating the direction of prayer. If you arrange a guide, ask them to point out the stone carvings and explain how the settlement used its position above the creek. Take a moment by the baobabs to look out over the water. National Museums of Kenya’s guide to Mnarani Ruins · Enzi Museum’s notes on the mosques and tombs

Allow roughly an hour for an unhurried look around. That’s a planning estimate, so leave extra time if you want a guided visit or breaks in the shade. Pack water and shoes with grip. If someone in your group uses a pushchair, wheelchair or walking aid, ask the museum which paths and viewpoints they can reach. Check opening hours, admission and guide arrangements before leaving.

Choose one stop after the ruins

  • Short on time? Go for lunch or a drink with a view of the creek and keep the onward drive flexible. Confirm whether your stop is on the Mnarani or Kilifi town side before setting off.
  • Want beach time? Choose a specific access point for Bofa Beach and arrange the return ride. Check conditions locally if you plan to swim. Kenya Tourism Board’s Kilifi coast guide
  • Want to get on the creek? Ask where the dhow boards, how long the outing takes and when you’ll be back. If departure is later in the day, leave space for lunch and a break between the ruins and the boat. Kenya Tourism Board’s dhow sailing guide

If you’re staying overnight in Kilifi

Choose your accommodation around the stop you care about most. A base on the Mnarani side can simplify a morning at the ruins. If your plans centre on Kilifi town or Bofa, ask your host for the nearest practical route. Compare the actual pins before booking, especially if you’ll be relying on rides.
