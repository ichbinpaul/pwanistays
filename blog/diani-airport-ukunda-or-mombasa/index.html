Ukunda or Mombasa? How to choose an airport for Diani Beach

PwaniStays · 27 November 2026

For a trip centred on Diani Beach, compare flights into Ukunda (UKA) first. It’s the nearby airstrip, so the final leg is a local transfer. If your itinerary already lands at Moi International Airport in Mombasa (MBA), price a direct transfer to Diani before adding another flight. Compare the time from your first landing to your accommodation entrance, including baggage, check-in and any airport change. Safarilink’s Diani route · Kenya Airways’ Mombasa airport information

If you’re connecting in Nairobi, check the airport code

Nairobi flights can leave from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) or Wilson Airport (WIL). Safarilink lists its Diani service from Wilson. If you arrive at Jomo Kenyatta and your onward flight departs from Wilson, allow for collecting bags, travelling between airports and checking in again. Check both departure airports for your date before you buy. Safarilink’s Wilson to Ukunda route · Jambojet’s Ukunda destination page

For flights on separate tickets, ask what happens if the first flight is delayed. Check the allowance and bag dimensions on the Diani leg before packing, especially if you’re carrying dive gear, a board or a pushchair. A flight with a cheaper fare can cost more by the time you add the extra transfer and bags.

If you land in Mombasa, confirm the drive to Diani

Ask the driver whether they plan to use the Dongo Kundu bypass or the Likoni ferry, and what they recommend for your arrival time. The bypass connects the mainland route to the south coast and gives drivers a way around Mombasa Island and the ferry crossing. If you’re stopping in Mombasa first, get the estimate from your actual pickup point. Kenya News Agency’s road route overview · Kenya Ports Authority’s ferry information

Ask for a door-to-door quote for your group and bags. Confirm where the driver waits, what happens if you land late and whether you’ll need a larger vehicle for luggage or a child seat. The return transfer can need a different pickup time, especially if you’re catching an early flight. Agree on that timing separately.

Send the entrance pin before you travel

Ask your host for the entrance or gate pin and a number the driver can call if needed. Include your airport code, flight number, scheduled landing time, passenger count and luggage in the same message. Keep the pin and pickup details on your phone so you can pull them up easily on arrival.

If you’re still choosing a place to stay, use the PwaniStays Diani accommodation guide to compare beachfront access, villa layouts and longer stays. If you’re also considering a stay in Nyali, the Nyali and Diani comparison can help you choose where to spend your nights.
