How we support owners

Property promotion

We present furnished coastal stays to guests with photos, amenities and practical details about the property.

Booking coordination

We help answer guest questions, check dates with you, prepare quotations and coordinate booking updates.

Management by arrangement

If you need help with day-to-day operations, we can discuss a separate management arrangement.

From first conversation to listing

  1. Share your property details

    Tell us where it’s located, what kind of unit it is, how many guests it sleeps and which amenities it has. Include current photos.

  2. Discuss pricing and availability

    We’ll talk through rates, availability, guest quotations and the commission or management arrangement that suits your property.

  3. Agree how we’ll work together

    Before listing, we agree in writing on responsibilities, payment handling, guest communication and property standards.
