Our story

A love for the coast.
A more personal way to experience it.

PwaniStays began with a simple belief: finding a coastal stay should come with the help of someone who knows the place. Local Kenyan knowledge and international travel experience came together around that idea.

We work with accommodation owners to help travellers compare apartments and villas, ask the right questions and request current rates. From Mombasa and Diani to Watamu and beyond, we help turn a shortlist into a stay that fits your plans.

Whether you’re planning a quiet beach break, time with family or a longer visit, our approach starts with listening. Tell us what matters to you, and we’ll help you explore your options.
