A three-day Mombasa itinerary with beach time and Old Town

PwaniStays · 22 November 2026

For a three-day Mombasa trip, give Old Town and Fort Jesus one unhurried morning, keep a full coastal day flexible, and leave the last outing close to your accommodation. The plan below assumes three days with at least two nights. If you arrive late, move the first morning to your first full day.

Day 1 at Fort Jesus and in Old Town

Start at Fort Jesus, then continue into Old Town on foot. Use the museum as a meeting point and choose a short walking route if you’re travelling with children or anyone who needs regular breaks. Confirm museum hours and admission before you leave. If you book a guide, agree on the route, duration and price first. Fort Jesus Museum · National Museums of Kenya Old Town map

Leave time for lunch, shade breaks and water stops along the walk. End with a relaxed afternoon around your accommodation. You can always return to town if you want to spend more time there.

Day 2 on the coast

Choose one main activity. For a low-key day, confirm the nearest public beach access from your stay and leave space for a long lunch. If you want a boat trip or snorkelling, Kenya Wildlife Service lists several access points, including Nyali Gate and Serena Gate. Ask your operator for the departure pin, when conditions are suitable for your group, and whether the quote includes park admission, equipment and life jackets. Mombasa Marine National Park and Reserve guide

Keep a land-based backup if water activities are postponed. Look at the local options in Coastal & Cultural Experiences and ask which activities work for your group and dates. Booking a second fixed activity on the same day leaves less room for a change in tides, weather or pace.

Day 3 near your accommodation

Start near your accommodation with a beach walk or another short outing. Confirm check-out time, luggage storage and your pickup point the evening before. If you’re taking the Madaraka Express, give your driver the pin for the Mombasa Terminus in Miritini. If you’re flying, give them your flight details. Ask when to leave and allow time for traffic. Kenya Railways boarding information

Where to base yourself

If the itinerary mixes town and north-coast beach time, Nyali is a useful area to compare. If most of your time will be around the north-coast beaches, look at Shanzu and confirm the transfer from your exact property. Pick a base that makes your first and last days easy, especially when arrival or departure falls outside daytime hours.

Make it your trip

For families, plan a morning outing and keep the afternoons flexible. For a group of adults, swap a quiet beach day for an organised coastal trip if everyone is keen. If you only have two full days, keep Old Town and the beach as your anchors and treat the final stop as optional.
