Where to stay in Malindi for town, beach and marine park visits

PwaniStays · 26 November 2026

For a short Malindi stay, start with where you want to spend your first full morning. Choose a town base if you want to visit the museums and seafront landmarks. Look around Silversands if you want easy access to the sand. If a marine park trip is the priority, compare stays along the Casuarina Road approach and pin your departure point before booking.

Stay near town for a morning of history

Malindi’s heritage stops are spread across several sites. The House of Columns and the Heritage Complex are named separately by the National Museums of Kenya, alongside the Portuguese Chapel and the Vasco da Gama Pillar. Save the pins you want to visit, since a driver may ask which museum building you mean. For a short morning, pick one museum building and the pillar. The pillar stands on the seafront and gives you a different view of the town’s former trading harbour. National Museums of Kenya’s Malindi Museum guide · Vasco da Gama Pillar visitor information

Ask your host what you can reach comfortably on foot and where to get a ride back. If you’re arriving late, check where you can eat near the stay and how check-in works. Confirm opening hours and admission directly with the museum before setting out.

Choose Silversands if you want more time on the beach

Silversands is along the coast near the Vasco da Gama Pillar. It makes sense to look here if you want a beach walk in the morning and time in town later. Ask for the walking route from the front door to the sand. With small children or anyone who finds steps difficult, ask if the route involves stairs, a road crossing or a stretch without shade. Malindi district map from Kenyatta University’s repository

If you’re comparing places advertised as beachfront, ask which gate or path guests use and whether you need to carry anything back from the beach. If swimming is part of the plan, check conditions on the day and ask locally where to enter. View a PwaniStays listing at Silversands

Use Casuarina Road as a reference for the marine park

Kenya Wildlife Service gives Casuarina Road as the road approach to Malindi Marine National Park and Reserve. Ask your operator for the exact meeting point, check-in time and whether transport or park admission is part of the quote. With a morning departure, check whether you can eat before leaving and when you’ll get back. Kenya Wildlife Service’s Malindi Marine National Park and Reserve guide · Kenya Tourism Board’s marine park listing

If the water trip can move to your second morning, keep a town visit as your backup. For a group split between a boat outing and a museum visit, compare the cost and convenience of two short rides with the extra cost of staying closer to either stop. View a PwaniStays listing at Casuarina Beach

Check three pins before you book

Save the accommodation entrance, the place you’ll start your main outing and somewhere you can eat when you get back. If you’ll be using a driver, send the pins together and ask about both legs of the trip.
